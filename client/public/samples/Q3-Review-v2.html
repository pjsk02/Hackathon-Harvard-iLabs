JT Morgan. Q3 2026 Regional Banking Review

Prepared by JT Morgan Finance for the Baine engagement team. Fictional company, demo document.

JT Morgan's regional branch network closed Q3 with $179.3M in revenue across four regions. Overall profitability held steady, but the gap between regions widened. The West and Northeast improved slightly on stronger deposit growth. The Midwest and South both declined, driven by heavier rate promotions and higher operating costs in smaller branches.

The South is now our weakest region at 16.2%, down almost two points since Q2. The Midwest follows at 18.7%. Both regions carry more branches per dollar of revenue than the coasts, which raises fixed costs.
